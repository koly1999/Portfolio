PRODUCT DESIGN · CAD · 3D PRINTING

Gimbal Protectors

3D-printed guards that protect the gimbals on RC controllers during transport. Hobby-grade gimbal sticks are expensive and easy to snag in a bag, so the guards cover them, grip them with an O-ring, and look good on the controller. This was some of my earliest CAD work, from early in high school, and the protectors sold well through HobbyTown USA.

SOLD AT HOBBYTOWN USA

Role
CAD design · validation testing · small-batch manufacturing
Process
ABS printing · 10 ft drop tests · fit & dimension iteration
Result
Sold through HobbyTown USA in multi-color batches, with an O-ring fit that grips the gimbal
